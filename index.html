An Arabic version of this policy is available on request. 02 — Information I Collect Information I Collect I collect only the information needed to receive, understand, and respond to your request. Information you provide Message content — the text and voice messages you send to the Assistant, including any details you choose to include such as account references, invoice numbers, or a description of your request. Contact identifiers — your WhatsApp phone number and, where available, your WhatsApp profile display name, which are used to route and respond to your conversation. Information collected automatically Message metadata — timestamps, message identifiers, and delivery or read status generated when a message is sent or received. Operational data — limited technical information required to process, transcribe, classify, and reply to your message. Please avoid sending sensitive data. The Assistant is not intended to collect special categories of personal data (such as health, religious, or financial-account credentials). Please share only the information necessary to resolve your request. 03 — How I Use Your Information How I Use Your Information I process your information solely to provide and improve the requested service, specifically to: Receive your message and, for voice notes, transcribe it into text so it can be understood; Interpret your request, identify its intent, and extract the details needed to act on it; Generate a relevant reply and, where you sent a voice note, respond in the same format; Create, track, and update records related to your request; Route your request to, or escalate it to, a human when required; Maintain the quality, reliability, and security of the Service, including diagnosing errors and preventing misuse. I do not use your information for advertising, and I do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. I do not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you without human involvement. 04 — Legal Basis for Processing Legal Basis for Processing Where data-protection law applies — including the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) — I rely on the following legal bases: Performance of a service — to respond to your request and deliver the customer-service function you initiated; Consent — where required, including your choice to begin and continue a conversation with the Assistant over WhatsApp. You may withdraw consent at any time; Legitimate interests — to operate, secure, and improve the Service, provided these interests are not overridden by your rights and freedoms; Legal obligation — where processing is necessary to comply with a legal or regulatory requirement. 05 — Data Retention Data Retention I keep personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy, and I apply the principle of data minimisation throughout the pilot. Voice audio is retained only briefly for transcription and is automatically deleted on a short lifecycle; Message transcripts and request records are retained only as long as needed to resolve and account for your request, or as required by law; Operational logs are retained for a limited period to support security and troubleshooting, then removed. Because the Assistant currently operates as a personal pilot, pilot data is subject to defined retention periods and is deleted at the conclusion of the pilot unless a longer period is required by law. When personal data is no longer needed, it is securely deleted or anonymised. 06 — Data Security Data Security I use industry-standard technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include: Encryption of data in transit between systems; Access controls and the principle of least privilege, so that data is accessible only where needed; Use of reputable, secured infrastructure and databases with restricted, authenticated access; Monitoring and logging to detect and respond to potential security events. Messages sent over WhatsApp are protected in transit by WhatsApp's own encryption. While I take security seriously, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and I cannot guarantee absolute security. 07 — Third-Party Services Third-Party Services The Assistant relies on trusted third-party providers to deliver its functionality.
